## 3. Drawing conventions

- **Units:** millimetres. Areas in m².
- **Origin:** west outer face (x = 0) and front outer face (y = 0). +y is assumed north. Dwelling B is the mirror of dwelling A: x′ = 18,600 − x.
- **Dimension key (also on the sheet):**
  - **External:** overall dimensions 18,600 × 12,300 and the chains outside the building.
  - **Clear internal:** room sizes in room labels and in the room schedule.
  - **Provisional:** every wall-thickness segment (300 external, 200 party, 100 partition), and therefore the overall dimensions; porch, patio, conservatory and path sizes.
- **Wall types:** external walls solid; party (separating) wall red with ANSI37 hatch; partitions solid grey. A legend states each thickness as provisional.
- **Scale:** true 1:50 on the A1 PDF. A 0–5 m scale bar is included for any other print size.
- **Dwellings:** labelled "DWELLING A" and "DWELLING B (MIRRORED)". The overall footprint is dimensioned and stated in the notes (18.60 × 12.30 m = 228.78 m²).
- **Doors and windows:** door leaves with swings on A-DOOR; glazing on A-GLAZ. Accessibility reference zones (turning circles, Diagram 2.5 bathroom zones) are on A-ACCS and are for reference only.

## 9. Known limitations of the deliverables

- **PNG route.** CADCraft's `plot` command wrote PDF data to a `.png` path, and its built-in PNG export is fixed at 2,400 × 1,600 px. The issued PNG was therefore rendered from the A1 vector PDF with the Windows PDF renderer (`Windows.Data.Pdf`). It is a faithful raster of the PDF.
- **DXF portability.** The DXF was written by CADCraft and checked by reopening it in CADCraft and with an independent parser. It has **not** been opened in AutoCAD or another CAD package. Text uses the "Standard" style, so fonts may look different elsewhere.
- **Model space only.** There is no paper-space layout or title-block border. The sheet is plotted from model extents at 1:50.
- **Indicative content.** Furniture and fittings are indicative. Accessibility zones are reference outlines, not a compliance demonstration.
- **Not produced.** Elevations, sections, roof plan, site plan and services layouts are not part of Draft 0.2.
- **Wall thicknesses.** All are placeholders. A thicker party wall or external wall would change the overall dimensions and the footprint.
