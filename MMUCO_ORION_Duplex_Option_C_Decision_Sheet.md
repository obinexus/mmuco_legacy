## 1. Accessibility: M4(1), M4(2), M4(3)

**Governing guidance.** Approved Document M Vol 1, 2015 edition incorporating 2016 amendments. Gov.uk lists it as current (page last updated 1 Oct 2024).

- **Para 0.3 (VERIFIED):** M4(2) and M4(3) are "optional requirements" that apply **only** where a planning condition imposes them. Without a condition, dwellings need only meet M4(1). Each dwelling is assessed against **one** category.
- **Para 0.5:** if an M4(3) condition does not specify (b), the dwelling defaults to M4(3)(2)(a), wheelchair *adaptable*.
- **Para 0.6:** building control must be told which optional requirements apply.

## 4. Ventilation: continuous mechanical extract (MEV) vs MVHR

**Common to both (ADF 2021, VERIFIED):**

- Whole-dwelling rate: the higher of 0.3 l/s × 104.13 m² = **31.2 l/s**, or 25 l/s (Table 1.3).
- Continuous high-rate extract: kitchen 13, bathroom 8, utility 8 l/s (Table 1.2).
- Purge ventilation by openable windows in every habitable room: 1/20 of floor area for windows opening ≥ 30° (Table 1.4).
- Noise guidance: ≤ 30 dB L<sub>Aeq,T</sub> in bedrooms and living rooms at low rate; ≤ 45 dB in kitchens and bathrooms (1.5–1.7).
- Maintenance access to filters, fans, coils and ducts (1.8).
- Commissioning (Section 4).

| | **Continuous MEV** (ADF 1.60–1.66) | **MVHR** (ADF 1.67–1.73) |
|---|---|---|
| Principle | Extract from the kitchen, bathroom and utility; fresh air enters through background ventilators | Balanced supply to every habitable room and extract from wet rooms, through a heat exchanger |
| Background ventilators | **Required:** ≥ 4,000 mm² per habitable room, not in wet rooms, at least bedrooms + 2 = **4 ventilators** (1.64) | **Must not be installed** (1.72) |
| Ducts (indicative) | 3 extract runs (kitchen, bathroom, utility) to one unit, plus one exhaust to outside | 5 supply runs (2 bedrooms, quiet room, living/dining, plus kitchen if specified) + 3 extract runs, plus intake and exhaust ducts to outside (insulated). Needs a roof void or bulkheads: **roof not designed** |
| Unit and space (ESTIMATE) | Small central unit, roughly 0.4 × 0.4 × 0.3 m; ceiling or cupboard | Roughly 0.6 × 0.6 × 0.3–0.6 m plus condensate drain; utility/plant room wall or loft. Fits the 2.5 × 2.0 m utility (tested plant allowance 0.6 × 0.6 m) |
| Maintenance (ESTIMATE) | Clean or replace filters and grilles about annually | Filters about every 6–12 months (manufacturer-dependent); duct cleaning access; summer-bypass check |
| Energy (ESTIMATE, illustrative only) | Fan about 10–20 W continuous. Ventilation heat loss at 31.2 l/s ≈ 37 W/K, roughly 1,600–1,800 kWh/yr of heat at about 2,000 K·days | Fans about 15–31 W (≈ 130–270 kWh/yr). Recovers roughly 75–90% of that ventilation heat (≈ 1,300–1,600 kWh/yr) **if the home is airtight**. Actual figures come from SAP/HEM and the product's tested data |
| Cost (ESTIMATE, not a quotation) | About £1.5k–£3k installed per home | About £4k–£8k installed per home; plus filters |
| Fit with Option C | Simple; trickle vents in every habitable room. Ground-floor bedroom ventilators and windows raise security and noise questions | Suits the internal bathroom; no trickle vents; good with closed windows at night. Purge windows are still required |
| Main risks | Draughts and noise through vents; lower energy performance | Needs airtightness and careful commissioning; ducts need a designed roof void; filter upkeep; condensate |

**Recommendation (not approved):** **MVHR** (also Research L75), subject to the energy model (SAP/HEM), the roof design for duct routes, and budget. Continuous MEV is the fallback. All performance and cost figures above are estimates to be replaced by design calculations and quotations.
