**Non-habitable rooms:**

- **Bathroom:** no external wall. ADF 1.48 requires an intermittent fan at four air changes per hour: 8.0 m² × 2.5 m = 20 m³, so 80 m³/h = **22.2 l/s**, which exceeds the 15 l/s in Table 1.1. ADF 1.51 requires a **15-minute overrun**. Alternatively, continuous extract at 8 l/s high rate.

- **Store and hall:** no extract required.

**Whole dwelling (ADF):** the higher of 0.3 l/s × 104.13 m² = **31.2 l/s** or 25 l/s for two bedrooms (Table 1.3), so **31.2 l/s**.

### 4.2 Overheating, ADO simplified method (screen)

- **Cross-ventilation:** yes. There are openings on the opposite south and north façades (ADO 1.4).
- **Glazing by façade (left dwelling):**
  - South: 3.24 m²
  - West: 4.32 m²
  - North: 5.49 m²
  - Total: **13.05 m², 12.5% of floor area**
- **Gardens to the north (as assumed):** the largest glazed façade is **north**.
  - Table 1.1 limits: whole dwelling 18% (moderate risk) or 15% (high risk); most glazed room 37%.
  - Result: 12.5% and the kitchen's 25.3% both **pass the screen**.
- **If the gardens face south:** the largest glazed façade becomes south.
  - Moderate risk: 15% and 30% limits; both pass.
  - High risk: the most-glazed-room limit is **22%**, so the kitchen at 25.3% **fails**, and shading under ADO 1.9 is also required.
- **Free area to remove excess heat (Table 1.3):**
  - Moderate risk: the greater of 9% of floor area (9.37 m²) or 55% of glazing (7.18 m²) = **9.37 m²**.
  - High risk: the greater of 6% of floor area (6.25 m²) or 70% of glazing (9.13 m²) = **9.13 m²**.
  - The design reaches 13.05 / 9.79 / 6.53 m² if 100% / 75% / 50% of glazed openings open fully.
  - **It passes only if at least about 75% of the glazing is fully openable** (casements or doors). Otherwise enlarge the openings or use dynamic modelling (CIBSE TM59).
- **Bedroom free area:** 4% of room area in moderate-risk locations (Bed 1 0.59 m², Bed 2 0.51 m²) or 13% in high-risk ones (1.92 / 1.66 m²). Because these are **ground-floor bedrooms, only openings that can be secured count at night** (ADO 3.6). That needs lockable louvred shutters, grilles or secure vents (3.7).
- **Location risk:** "high risk" covers urban and some suburban parts of London (ADO Appendix C). The site is unknown. If it is the Phase 1 area (IG6, London), check Appendix C.

**Trade-offs**

- **M4(1)**
  - C already meets it with margin.
  - It is the lowest cost, but falls short of MMUCO ORION's "accessible dwellings" intent (MMUCO ORION.pdf p.12–14; Roadmap 3A "all buildings wheelchair accessible").
- **M4(2)**
  - Achievable **within the current 104.13 m² GIA**.
  - It needs: a bath (with level-shower provision); an outward-opening or sliding bathroom door; principal-window sills ≤ 850 mm; reinforced bathroom walls; and services at 450–1,200 mm.
  - It is the best fit with "adaptable" and "independent living" at little extra cost.
- **M4(3)(a)** adaptable, or **(b)** accessible
  - Feasible in C's plan form, but needs:
    - Extra storage and transfer space (about +2 m²).
    - A Bedroom 1 re-plan (tested and works).
    - A larger bathroom to Diagram 3.10 / 3.11.
    - Worktop lengths (Tables 3.3 / 3.4) and the combined living/dining/kitchen minimum (Table 3.2), which I have not been able to read reliably.
    - A hoist-ready ceiling.
  - Estimated **+3 to 6 m² GIA per home**, likely still ≤ 110 m². Highest cost and specification burden.

**My recommendation, not a selection:** design **both homes to M4(2) as the base**, keeping the M4(3)-ready features C already has (1.5 m hall, 850 mm doors, 1.5 m kitchen clearance). Make one or both homes **M4(3)(a)** if a wheelchair user is expected or the planning authority requires it. **Decision needed: bath (M4(2)) or installed level-access shower (M4(3) / r52–53).**
